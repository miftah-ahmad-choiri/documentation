## **Course overview**

Course ES42117 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the Diamanti SR630 appliance machine type 7X02CTO8WW.

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the Diamanti SR630 appliance, the support flow of the appliance and hardware replacement procedures.

**Course objectives**
- Describe the Diamanti SR630 overview
- Describe the Diamanti SR630 support flow
- Describe the Diamanti SR630 hardware replacement procedures

**Topics** 
- Topic 1 – Introducing the Diamanti SR630  
- Topic 2 – Support flow 
- Topic 3 – Hardware replacement procedures 
- Topic 4 – End-of-course summary
