## **Course overview**

Course ES62719 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem DG storage systems (machine types: 7DHY, 7DHZ, and 7DJ9).The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ThinkSystem DG Series storage system specifications, components, system configurations, management tools, and the problem determination procedures.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the ThinkSystem DG5200, DG7200, DG242N, and their components
- List the features and specifications
- Describe the cable connections for the DG5200, DG7200, and DG242N
- Provide an update of the ONTAP software bundle
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the DG storage systems
- Describe the FRU replacement procedures
- Describe the software and firmware upgrade procedures

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem DG Series storage systems 
- Topic 2 – Features and specifications  
- Topic 3 – DG Series cabling and configuration 
- Topic 4 – ONTAP software bundle update 
- Topic 5 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 6 – Hardware replacement tips 
- Topic 7 – DG Series firmware updates
