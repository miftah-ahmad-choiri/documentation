## **Course overview**

Course ES72684 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem ST45 V3 (machine types: 7DH4, 7DH5).

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ST45 V3 specifications, components, system configuration, and management tools. By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Objectives**
* Describe the Lenovo ThinkSystem ST45 V3 server and components
* List the ST45 V3 specifications
* Describe the ST45 V3 configurations and block diagrams
* Describe the ST45 V3 management tools
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the ST45 V3


**Prerequisites** 
* [ES42190](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES42190) – Servicing the ThinkSystem 4350/5350/9350 RAID/HBA series adapters  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Product overview 
* Topic 2 – ST45 V3 system components 
* Topic 3 – System configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 5 – Hardware replacement tips 
* Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
