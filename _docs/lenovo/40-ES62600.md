Machine type(s): 7DE4, 7DE5, 7Y62 <br>
Course duration: 120 minutes

## **Course overview**

Course ES62600 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain ThinkSystem DG Series storage systems (machine types 7DE4, 7DE5, and 7Y62).

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ThinkSystem DG Series storage system specifications, components, system configurations, management tools, and the problem determination procedures.

This course update introduces the DG240N expansion enclosure for DG5000/DG7000 controllers.

By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course objectives**

* Describe the DG Series storage controllers and their components
* Describe the DG240N expansion enclosure and its components
* List the features and specifications
* Describe the cable connections for the DG Series
* Provide an update on the ONTAP software bundle
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the DG Series
* Describe the FRU replacement procedures

**Prerequisites** 
* ES71914C - Servicing the Lenovo ThinkSystem DM Series storage systems 
* ES42026 - ONTAP 9.7 Administration 
* ES51592 - Servicing the Lenovo ThinkSystem DM7100 Series and the DM240N 
* ES52101 - Servicing the Lenovo ThinkSystem DM5100F storage system 

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem DG Series storage systems 
* Topic 2 – Features and specifications 
* Topic 3 – DG Series cabling and configuration 
* Topic 4 – DM Series cabling and power on/off procedures 
* Topic 5 – ONTAP software bundle update 
* Topic 6 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 7 – Hardware replacement tips
