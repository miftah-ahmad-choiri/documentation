## **Course overview**

Course ES52101 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem DM5100F storage system

The intent of this course is to familiarize the Level 1 service personnel with the product components, features and specifications, cable connections, management tools, command-line operations, and the problem determination procedures.

By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the ThinkSystem DM5100F and its components
- List the features and specifications
- Describe how to connect the cables in the DM5100F
- Provide an overview of ONTAP 9.8
- Describe the FRU replacement procedures
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the ThinkSystem DM5100F
