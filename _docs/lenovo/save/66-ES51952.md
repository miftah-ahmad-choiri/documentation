## **Course overview**

Course ES51952 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem DM7100 Series storage controllers and the DM240N expansion unit.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the ThinkSystem DM7100 Series storage controllers and the DM240N expansion unit and components
- List the features and specifications
- Describe how to connect the cables in the DM7100 Series
- Describe the FRU replacement procedures
- Provide an overview of ONTAP 9.7
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the ThinkSystem DM7100 Series

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem DM7100 series storage controller 
- Topic 2 – Features and specifications 
- Topic 3 – DM7100 series cabling 
- Topic 4 – ONTAP 9.7 overview 
- Topic 5 – FRU replacement 
- Topic 6 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 7 – End-of-course summary
