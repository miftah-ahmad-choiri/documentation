## **Course overview**

Course ES72642 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SR650 V4 and SR650a V4 (machine types: 7DGC, 7DGD, 7DGE, 7DGF, 7DK2, and 7DLN).

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the SR650 V4 and SR650a V4 specifications, components, system configurations, management tools, and problem determination techniques.

By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course objectives**
- Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem SR650 V4 and SR650a V4
- Identify the components of the SR650 V4 and SR650a V4
- Describe the configurations of the SR650 V4 and SR650a V4
- Describe the SR650 V4 and SR650a V4 management tools
- Describe the specific problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the SR650 V4 and SR650a V4


**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – ThinkSystem SR650 V4 and SR650a V4 overview 
- Topic 2 – ThinkSystem SR650 V4 and SR650a V4 system configurations and diagrams 
- Topic 3 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 4 – Hardware replacement tips 
- Topic 5 – End-of-course summary
