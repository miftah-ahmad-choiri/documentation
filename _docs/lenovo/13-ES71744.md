Course code: [ES71744](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?id=220) <br>
Machine type(s): 7X01 7X02 <br>
Course duration: 45 minutes <br>

## **Course overview**

Course ES71744 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server.
The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server specifications, components, and system configuration. By viewing the videos, students will learn how to install and remove the system components.

**Course objectives** <br>
Students will be able to:
* Describe the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server and components.
* List the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server specifications.
* Describe the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server configurations and diagrams.


**Prerequisites** <br> 
Before you begin, take the following prerequisites for this course. You must also have knowledge of high-performance computing (HPC) systems, be familiar with the general administration of a high-volume, multicore server, and have problem determination and troubleshooting skills. 
* [ES41758](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41758) - ThinkSystem servers architecture introduction  
* [ES51757](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757) - Introducing ThinkSystem tools  
* [ES41759](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759) - ThinkSystem problem determination  
* [ES51780](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51780) - Servicing the ThinkSystem storage controller  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server and components. 
* Topic 2 – Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server specifications. 
* Topic 3 – Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 MT 7X01 and MT 7X02 server configurations and diagrams. 
* Topic 4 – End of course summary.
