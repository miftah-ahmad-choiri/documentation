## **Course overview**

Course ES42545 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the NVIDIA Mellanox SN5600, which is part of the EveryScale solution.

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the Mellanox switch specifications, components, system configurations, initial installation, and problem determination procedures.

**Objectives**

* Describe the Mellanox SN5600 and its components
* Describe the features and specifications of the SN5600
* Describe Cumulus Linux concepts and management commands
* Describe the initial power-on procedures and configuration of the SN5600
* Describe the problem determination and troubleshooting procedures for the SN5600

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Product overview 
* Topic 2 – Features and specifications 
* Topic 3 – Cumulus Linux overview 
* Topic 4 – Initial power on and configuration 
* Topic 5 – Cumulus OS NVUE and ports configuration 
* Topic 6 – Backing up and upgrading Cumulus Linux 
* Topic 7 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 8 – End-of-course summary
