## **Course overview**

Course ES41722 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the Lenovo ThinkSystem NE2552E Flex Switch.

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ThinkSystem NE2552E Flex Switch, describe the switch configuration and management, and explain how to update the switch firmware. Switch removal and replacement procedures along with problem determination and troubleshooting steps are also provided.

**Course objectives**

- Provide an overview of the Lenovo ThinkSysem NE2552E Flex Switch.
- Describe the ThinkSystem QLogic Ethernet adapter options.
- Describe the features and specifications of the switch.
- Explain how to configure the switch.
- Explain how to update the firmware.
- List the removal and replacement steps.
- List the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot the ThinkSystem NE2552E Flex Switch.

**Topics** 
- Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem NE2552E Flex Switch 
- Topic 2 – ThinkSystem QLogic Ethernet adapter options 
- Topic 3 – ThinkSystem NE2552E Flex Switch features and specifications 
- Topic 4 – System configurations and diagrams 
- Topic 5 – Removal and replacement procedures  
- Topic 6 – Problem determination and troubleshooting  
- Topic 7 – End of course summary
