## **Course overview**

Course ES42714 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the different Neptune Liquid Cooling systems.
The intent of this course is to provide an overview of Neptune Liquid Cooling systems, including DWCMs, and to introduce the different liquid cooling technologies, components, management tools, and problem determination and replacement techniques.

**Course Objectives**

* Describe Neptune Liquid Cooling systems
* List the servers that support Neptune Liquid Cooling systems
* Describe the different types of Neptune Liquid Cooling systems
* Describe Neptune Liquid Cooling components
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with Neptune Liquid Cooling systems

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES51757B](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757B) – Introducing ThinkSystem tools  
* [ES52374](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES52374) – ThinkSystem tools for the ThinkSystem V3 platform 
* [ES52678](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES52678) – ThinkSystem tools for the ThinkSystem V4 platform  
* [ES41759C](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759C) – ThinkSystem problem determination 

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Neptune Liquid Cooling overview 
* Topic 2 – Neptune Liquid Cooling rack 
* Topic 3 – Neptune Liquid Cooling solutions 
* Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 5 – Hardware replacement tips 
* Topic 6 – Resources 
* Topic 7 – End-of-course summary
