## **Course overview**

Course ES42190 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the the ThinkSystem 4350/5350/9350 RAID/HBA series adapters.

The course provides an overview of the new Lenovo RAID/HBA adapters’ features, specifications, and management tools.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the features of the new Lenovo RAID/HBA adapters
- Identify the differences between the RAID/HBA adapters
- Describe how to configure RAID adapters with different tools
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the new Lenovo RAID/HBA adapters


**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Product overview 
- Topic 2 – Features and specifications 
- Topic 3 – System configurations and diagrams 
- Topic 4 – Lenovo system tools support and RAID setup 
- Topic 5 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
