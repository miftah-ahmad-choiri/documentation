## **Course overview**

Course ES72751 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SR680a V3 with B200 (machine type: 7DM9 and 7DP9).
The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the SR680a V3 with B200 specifications, components, system configurations, and management tools. By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the ThinkSystem SR680a V3 with B200 and its components
- List the SR680a V3 with B200 specifications
- Describe the SR680a V3 with B200 configurations
- Describe the SR680a V3 with B200 management tools
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the SR680a V3 with B200

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Product overview 
- Topic 2 – SR680a V3 with B200 system components
- Topic 3 – System configurations and diagrams 
- Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 5 – Hardware replacement tips 
- Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
