## **Course overview**

Course ES41551 provides updated technical information to service personnel who are required to service and maintain the x3550 M5, x3650 M5, nx360 M5, x240 M5, TD350, RD350, RD450, RD550, and RD650.
The intent of this course is to highlight the changes made to the machines with the added support of the most recent Intel Xeon E5-2600 v4 processor.

**Course objectives**

- Identify the changes made to the ThinkServer servers TD350, RD350, RD450, RD550, RD650, NeXtScale nx360 M5, Flex x240 M5, System x servers x3550 M5, and x3650 M5 that support the most recent Intel Xeon E5-2600 v4 processor.


**Topics** 
- Topic 1 – New CPU 
- Topic 2 – New DIMM support 
- Topic 3 – New machine types 
- Topic 4 – New logo 
- Topic 5 – New chassis components 
- Topic 6 – Updated OS support 
- Topic 7 – Lenovo XClarity Administrator support 
- Topic 8 – Detailed changes of nx360 M5 
- Topic 9 – Detailed changes of x240 M5 
- Topic 10 – Detailed changes of x3550 M5 and x3650 M5 
- Topic 11 – Detailed changes of TD350/RD350/RD450/RD550/RD650 
- Topic 12 – End of course summary
