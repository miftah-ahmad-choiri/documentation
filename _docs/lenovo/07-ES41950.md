## **Course overview**

Course ES41950 provides updated technical information to service personnel who are required to service and maintain ThinkSystem servers with Intel second-generation Xeon Scalable processors.
The intent of this course is to highlight the changes made to the machines with the added support of the most recent Intel Xeon Scalable processors.

**Course objectives** <br>
Students will be able to:
* Describe the features of Intel second-generation Xeon Scalable processors
* Describe the updated ThinkSystem specifications
* Describe the updated ThinkSystem tools features


**Prerequisites** 
* [**ES41758**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41758) – ThinkSystem servers architecture introduction 
* [**ES51757**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757) – Introducing ThinkSystem tools 

**Topics**  
* Topic 1 – Intel second-generation Xeon Scalable processors overview 
* Topic 2 – ThinkSystem servers updated specification 
* Topic 3 – ThinkSystem tools updated features 
* Topic 4 – End of course summary
