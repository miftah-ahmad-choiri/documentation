## **Course overview**

Course ES72608 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem ST250 V3 (machine types: 7DCE, 7DCF and 7DCG).

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ST250 V3 specifications, components, system configurations, and management tools. By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course Objectives**

* Describe the ThinkSystem ST250 V3 and its components
* List the ST250 V3 specifications
* Describe the ST250 V3 configurations and block diagrams
* Describe the ST250 V3 management tools
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the ST250 V3

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES42373B](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES42373B) – Intel Xeon processor architecture for ThinkSystem V3 servers  
* [ES51757B](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757B) – Introducing ThinkSystem tools  
* [ES52374](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES52374) – ThinkSystem tools for the ThinkSystem V3 platform  
* [ES41759C](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759C) – ThinkSystem problem determination   
* [ES51780C](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51780C) – Servicing ThinkSystem storage controllers   
* [ES42190](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES42190) – Servicing the ThinkSystem 4350/5350/9350 RAID/HBA series adapters  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem ST250 V3 
* Topic 2 – ST250 V3 specifications 
* Topic 3 – ST250 V3 configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 4 – Parts replacement tips 
* Topic 5 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
