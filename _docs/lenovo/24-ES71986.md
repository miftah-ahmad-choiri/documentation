Machine type(s): 7D2F 7D2G 7D2H <br>
Course duration: 90 minutes

## **Course overview**

Course ES71986 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SR850P server, machine types 7D2F, 7D2G, and 7D2H. The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the product and information about the architecture and configuration of the system. <br>
By watching the videos, service personnel will also become familiar with the ThinkSystem SR850P replacement procedures.

**Course objectives** <br>
Students will be able to:
* Describe the features of the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850P
* Describe the specifications of the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850P
* Describe the components of the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850P
* Describe the configurations of the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850P
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850P

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES41758](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41758) - ThinkSystem servers architecture introduction  
* [ES51757](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757) - Introducing ThinkSystem tools  
* [ES41759](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759) - ThinkSystem problem determination  
* [ES41950](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41950) - ThinkSystem servers with Intel second-generation Xeon Scalable processors 
* [ES51965](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51965) – Introducing the Intel DC persistent memory module  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850P server 
* Topic 2 – Server features and specifications 
* Topic 3 – System diagram 
* Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 5 – End-of-course summary
