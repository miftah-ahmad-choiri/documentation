Machine type(s): 7D8T
Course duration: 60 minutes


## **Course overview**

Course ES52328B provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkEdge SE450 machine type 7D8T. This course update includes details about the new Root of Trust (RoT) module and enablement of the Security Pack feature.

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the SE450 specifications, components, system configuration, and problem determination procedures. By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course objectives**
- Describe the ThinkEdge SE450 and its components
- Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkEdge SE450
- Describe the different PCIe and storage configurations of the ThinkEdge SE450
- Describe the scope of ThinkShield support on the ThinkEdge SE450
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the ThinkEdge SE450

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – What’s new 
- Topic 2 – Introducing the SE450 
- Topic 3 – ThinkEdge SE450 specifications 
- Topic 4 – Configuration and diagrams  
- Topic 5 – ThinkShield and SED support on the SE450 
- Topic 6 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 7 – End-of-course summary
