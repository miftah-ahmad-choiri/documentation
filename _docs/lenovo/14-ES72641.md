## **Course overview**

Course ES72641 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SR630 V4 (machine type: 7DG8, 7DG9, 7DGA, 7DGB, and 7DK1).

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the SR630 V4 specifications, components, system configuration, management tools, and problem determination techniques.

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES42692](https://lenovoedu.Lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES42692) – Intel Xeon processor architecture for ThinkSystem V4 servers  
* [ES52678](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES52678) – ThinkSystem tools for the ThinkSystem V4 platform  
* [ES41759C](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759C) – Introducing ThinkSystem problem determination  

**Course objectives Students will be able to**: 
* Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem SR630 V4  
* Identify the components of the SR630 V4  
* Describe the configurations of the SR630 V4  
* Describe the SR630 V4 server management tools 
* Describe the specific problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the SR630 V4  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – ThinkSystem SR630 V4 overview 
* Topic 2 – ThinkSystem SR630 V4 system configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 3 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 4 – Hardware replacement tips Topic 5 – End-of-course summary
