## **Course overview**

Course ES71764 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain ThinkSystem ST550 (MT 7X09 and MT 7X10). The course provides an overview of ST550, chassis configurations, and PCIe adapter Installations.

**Course objectives**
* Describe the specifications of the ThinkSystem ST550.
* Explain storage and chassis configuration rules of the ThinkSystem ST550.
* Explain PCIe adapter installation rules of the ThinkSystem ST550.

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES41758](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41758) - ThinkSystem servers architecture introduction  
* [ES51757](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757) - Introducing ThinkSystem tools   
* [ES41759](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759) - ThinkSystem problem determination  
* [ES51780](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51780) - Servicing the ThinkSystem storage controllers  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the Lenovo ThinkSystem ST550 
* Topic 2 – Configuring the Lenovo ThinkSystem ST550 
* Topic 3 – PCIe adapter installation rules 
* Topic 4 – End of course summary
