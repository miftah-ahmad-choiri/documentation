## **Course overview**

Course ES72720 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SR860 V4 (machine types: 7DJQ, 7DJN, 7DJR). The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the SR860 V4 specifications, components, system configurations, management tools, and problem determination techniques.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem SR860 V4
- Identify the components of the SR860 V4
- Describe the configurations of the SR860 V4
- Describe the SR860 V4 hardware replacement tips
- Describe the specific problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the SR860 V4

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – ThinkSystem SR860 V4 overview 
- Topic 2 – ThinkSystem SR860 V4 system configurations and diagrams 
- Topic 3 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 4 – Hardware replacement tips 
- Topic 5 – End-of-course summary
