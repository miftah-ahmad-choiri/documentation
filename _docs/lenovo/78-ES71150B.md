## **Course overview**

Course ES71150B provides technical information to service personnel who are required to service and maintain the System x3850 X6 and System x3950 X6.
The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the system’s features and the in-depth architecture information of the system. This course compares the current product with the predecessor product and provides an overview of the changes and enhancements. This course includes scaling from x3850 X6 to x3950 X6 and describes the system reboot numbers derived from maintenance activities.
By viewing the videos, you will be able to perform the installation and removal procedures for the system components.

**Course objectives**
- Provide an overview of the System x3850 X6 and x3950 X6.
- Describe the product specifications and the new technology of System x3850 X6 and x3950 X6
- Identify the differences between the System x3850 X5 and the System x3850 X6.
- Explain how to perform problem determination activities and troubleshoot the System x3850 X6 and x3950 X6.
- Explain how to scale from System x3850 X6 to x3950 X6.
- Explain how many reboots will occur after performing specific maintenance activities.

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Introducing the System x3850 X6  
- Topic 2 – Configuring the System x3850 X6  
- Topic 3 – Scaling from x3850 X6 to x3950 X6  
- Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 5 – System Reboots Topic 6 – End of course summary
