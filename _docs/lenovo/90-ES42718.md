## **Course overview**

Course ES42718 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem DB710S FC SAN Switch (machine type: 7DHN). The course includes an overview of the system and its architecture.

**Course objectives**
- Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem DB710S Fibre Channel (FC) SAN Switch (7DHN)
- Describe the differences between the DB710S, DB720S, and DB730S series models
- Describe the features of the SANnav management software
- Describe the features of the Fabric OS commands and Fabric Web Tools
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the DB710S FC SAN Switch

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Product overview 
- Topic 2 – ThinkSystem DB710S features and specifications 
- Topic 3 – Brocade SANnav Management Portal and SANnav Global View 
- Topic 4 – Initial power on and configuration 
- Topic 5 – Fabric OS Web Tools 
- Topic 6 – Fabric OS commands 
- Topic 7 – Updating Fabric OS 
- Topic 8 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 9 – End-of-course summary
