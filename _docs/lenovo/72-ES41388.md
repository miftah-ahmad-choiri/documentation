## **Course overview**

Course ES41388 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the Lenovo eXFlash DIMM Memory-Channel Storage.
The intent of this course is to familiarize level 1 personnel with the product components and the problem determination procedures for the system.

**Course objectives**

- Describe the features of the Lenovo eXFlash DIMM Memory-Channel Storage.
- Provide an overview of the Lenovo eXFlash DIMM Memory-Channel Storage.
- Describe the product specifications and the new technology of the Lenovo eXFlash DIMM Memory-Channel Storage.
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot the Lenovo eXFlash DIMM Memory-Channel Storage.

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Lenovo eXFlash DIMM Overview 
- Topic 2 – Features and specifications 
- Topic 3 – Problem determination and troubleshooting  
- Topic 4 – End of course summary
