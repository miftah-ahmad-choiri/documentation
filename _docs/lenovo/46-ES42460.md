## **Course overview**

Course ES42460 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the Lenovo ThinkAgile VX Series integrated system and certified nodes with the VMware vSphere Distributed Services Engine.

The course update includes dual DPU support information for the ThinkSystem SR650 V3 and ThinkAgile VX650 V3.

The intent of this course is to familiarize service personnel with the VMware vSAN product components, features and specifications, and the problem determination procedures for the system.

**Course Objectives**

* Provide an overview of the VMware vSphere Distributed Services Engine on Lenovo ThinkAgile VX and ThinkSystem platform
* Describe the VMware vSphere Distributed Services Engine on Lenovo ThinkAgile VX and ThinkSystem solution and how this solution works
* Describe the service flow
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the VMware vSphere Distributed Services Engine on Lenovo ThinkAgile VX and ThinkSystem platform

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES41800E](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41800E) -  Servicing the Lenovo ThinkAgile VX Series 

**Topics**  
* Topic 1 – What’s new – Dual DPU support  
* Topic 2 – Overview of VMware vSphere Distributed Services Engine on Lenovo ThinkAgile VX  
* Topic 3 – Supported platform and configurations 
* Topic 4 – How the solution works and the life cycle management 
* Topic 5 – Problem determination and troubleshooting  
* Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
