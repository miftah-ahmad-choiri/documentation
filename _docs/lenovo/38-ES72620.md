## **Course overview**

Course ES72620 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SD530 V3 (machine types: 7DD3, 7DDA), SD550 V3 (machine types: 7DD2, 7DD9), and D3 chassis (machine types: 7DD0, 7DD7).

The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ThinkSystem SD530 V3, SD550 V3, and D3 chassis specifications, components, system configurations, management tools, and the problem determination procedures.

**Course objectives**

* Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem D3 chassis
* Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem SD550 V3
* Describe the configurations of the SD550 V3
* Describe the features and specifications of the ThinkSystem SD530 V3
* Describe the configurations of the SD530 V3
* Describe the server management tools
* Describe the specific problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the SD530 V3 and SD550 V3

**Prerequisites**  
* ES42373 – Intel Xeon processor architecture for ThinkSystem V3 servers 
* ES51757B – Introducing ThinkSystem tools 
* ES52374 – ThinkSystem tools for the ThinkSystem V3 platform 
* ES41759C – Introducing ThinkSystem problem determination 
* ES51780C - Servicing ThinkSystem storage controllers 
* ES42190 - Servicing the ThinkSystem 4350/5350/9350 RAID/HBA series adapters 

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem D3 chassis  
* Topic 2 – SD550 V3 features and specifications 
* Topic 3 – SD550 V3 configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 4 – SD530 V3 features and specifications 
* Topic 5 – SD530 V3 configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 6 – Management tools
