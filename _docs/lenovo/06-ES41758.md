## **Course overview**

Course ES41758 provides architecture information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain ThinkSystem servers.

**Students will be able to**:
* Describe the key components and technology of the Purley platforms.
* Identify the differences between the Grantley, Brickland, and Purley platforms.
* Describe the different processor levels, features, and configurations.
* Identify ThinkSystem servers supported memory and storage features.

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – ThinkSystem overview 
* Topic 2 – ThinkSystem processor features and configurations  
* Topic 3 – Memory and storage introduction 
* Topic 4 – End of course summary
