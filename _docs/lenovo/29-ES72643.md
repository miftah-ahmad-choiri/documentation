## **Course overview**

Course ES72643 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem ST50 V3 (machine types: 7DF3 and 7DF4). The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the ST50 V3 specifications, components, system configurations, and management tools. By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course Objectives**
* Describe the ThinkSystem ST50 V3 features and specifications
* Describe the ST50 V3 configurations
* Describe the ST50 V3 management tools
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the ST50 V3

**Prerequisites** 
* [**ES42373B**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES42373B) – Intel Xeon processor architecture for ThinkSystem V3 servers  
* [**ES51757B**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757B) – Introducing ThinkSystem tools  
* [**ES52374**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES52374) – ThinkSystem tools for the ThinkSystem V3 platform  
* [**ES41759C**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759C) – Introducing ThinkSystem problem determination  
* [**ES51780C**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51780C) – Servicing ThinkSystem storage controllers  
* [**ES42190**](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES42190) – Servicing the ThinkSystem 4350/5350/9350 RAID/HBA series adapters  

**Topics**  
* Topic 1 – Product overview 
* Topic 2 – System configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 3 – Management tools  
* Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
* Topic 5 – Hardware replacement tips
