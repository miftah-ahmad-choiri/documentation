## **Course overview**

Course ES42728 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem 4450/5450/9450 RAID/HBA series adapters.
The course provides an overview of the adapter features, specifications, and management tools.

**Course Objectives**
* Describe the features of the ThinkSystem 4450/5450/9450 RAID/HBA adapters
* Identify the differences between the RAID/HBA adapters
* Describe how to configure RAID adapters with different tools
* Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the RAID/HBA adapters

**Prerequisites**  
* [ES51757B](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757B) - Introducing ThinkSystem tools  
* [ES41759C](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?id=993) - ThinkSystem problem determination  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Product overview 
* Topic 2 – Features and specifications 
* Topic 3 – System configurations and diagrams 
* Topic 4 – Lenovo system tool support and RAID/HBA setup 
* Topic 5 – Problem determination and troubleshooting  
* Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
