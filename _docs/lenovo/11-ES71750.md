Course code: [ES71750](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?id=215) <br>
Machine type(s): 7X03 7X04 <br>
Course duration: 30 minutes <br>

**Course overview**

Course ES71750 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR550 (MT 7X03 and MT 7X04). The course provides an overview of the SR550, memory and storage configurations, and network adapter options.

**Course objectives** <br>
Students will be able to:
* Describe the specifications of the ThinkSystem SR550.
* Explain memory and storage configuration rules of the ThinkSystem SR550.
* Describe the support options of LOM, ML2, and PCIe adapters.

**Prerequisites** 
* [ES41758](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41758) - ThinkSystem servers architecture introduction  
* [ES51757](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51757) - Introducing ThinkSystem tools   
* [ES41759](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES41759) - ThinkSystem problem determination  
* [ES51780](https://lenovoedu.lenovo.com/course/view.php?idnumber=ES51780) - Servicing the ThinkSystem storage controllers  

**Topics** 
* Topic 1 – Introducing the ThinkSystem SR550 
* Topic 2 – Configuring the ThinkSystem SR550 
* Topic 3 – Networking adapter options 
* Topic 4 – End of course summary
