## **Course overview**

Course ES72721 provides technical information for service personnel who are required to service and maintain the ThinkSystem SR850 V4 (machine types: 7DJS, 7DJT, and 7DJU).
The intent of this course is to provide an overview of the SR850 V4 specifications, components, system configuration, and management tools. By watching the videos, you will learn how to install and remove system components.

**Course objectives**
- Describe the Lenovo ThinkSystem SR850 V4 server and components
- List the SR850 V4 specifications
- Describe the SR850 V4 configurations and block diagrams
- Describe the SR850 V4 management tools
- Describe the problem determination steps and explain how to troubleshoot issues with the SR850 V4

**Topics**  
- Topic 1 – Product overview 
- Topic 2 – SR850 V4 system components 
- Topic 3 – System configurations and diagrams 
- Topic 4 – Problem determination and troubleshooting 
- Topic 5 – Hardware replacement tips 
- Topic 6 – End-of-course summary
